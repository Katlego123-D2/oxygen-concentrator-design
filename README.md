# oxygen-concentrator-design
Mechanical engineering final year project: Design and fabrication of an oxygen concentrator

## Project Overview

This project was undertaken as part of my final-year Mechanical Engineering studies. It involved the design and fabrication of an oxygen concentrator.

The project provided an opportunity to apply mechanical engineering principles, technical design, modelling and simulation to develop a practical engineering solution.

## Engineering Software

- **SolidWorks:** Used for mechanical design and 3D modelling.
- **MATLAB/Simulink:** Used for engineering modelling and simulation.

## Project Development

The project involved conceptual design, engineering analysis, system modelling and the fabrication of a physical prototype.
